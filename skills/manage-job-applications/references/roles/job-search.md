# Job Search

Find current, suitable vacancies and deliver a verified preparation queue. Read the user's private profile, target markets, exclusions, research index, tracker and unfinished applications first. Consult [regional research](../../resources/README.md) and the relevant Schengen, Slovakia or India/Western resource. Adapt the same method to any other requested country.

## Work

1. Reconcile unfinished applications and saved jobs before discovering new roles. Search by title variants, skills, adjacent job families, seniority, location and working language. Combine official employer careers pages with appropriate local, specialist and general boards. Use search operators or public ATS search where supported, then open the actual listing.
2. Verify the full current JD through the employer or its linked ATS. Record employer, requisition, canonical URL, job title, location, working language, employment type, posting/closing date when stated, requirements and checked timestamp. A blocked page remains unverified. A directory entry alone does not establish an active vacancy.
3. Deduplicate by employer and requisition across all regions and trackers, falling back to canonical URL only when no requisition exists. Check prior submissions and unresolved attempts before adding work.
4. Build a requirement-to-evidence comparison. Separate mandatory qualifications, language, country-specific work rights and essential experience from preferences. A high score cannot clear a failed mandatory requirement. Verify current sponsorship or regulatory claims through official sources when needed and distinguish a sponsor's history from support for this vacancy.
5. Estimate fit using skills 30, relevant experience 25, education 15, location/eligibility 15 and seniority/responsibilities 15. Show awarded points, supporting candidate evidence and gaps. Keep the user's configured threshold and exceptions. Unknown evidence earns no assumed match. Label this a manual fit estimate.
6. Route eligible roles to CV Creation. Preserve holds, review uncertain cases and record concrete reasons for skips. Keep regional search results in the user's chosen private workspace.

## Handoff to CV Creation

Provide the employer/requisition key, full JD snapshot and source/check date, candidate source references, requirement/evidence matrix, fit breakdown, gaps, target region, output/editor requirements and current tracker row identity. Set `Ready to prepare` only when the sources support preparation. Keep application state separate.

Own discovery and fit assessment. Report newly verified roles, duplicate skips, closures, missing evidence and next actions. Claims about rankings, conversion rates or platform algorithms in research require verification before being presented as established facts.
