## Add Gemini or other research

1. Ask the user for the research file or chosen Drive folder, and inspect accessible documents before requesting another upload. Preserve original documents in their selected workspace.
2. Record document title, returned source reference, author or research provider, document date, inspection date, region, role, supported claims, cited primary sources and unresolved questions in the private research inventory. Mark partial reads and missing citations.
3. Extract actionable methods into the relevant resource or private working supplement. Retain source provenance with each substantive claim. Personal preferences belong in that user's rules file. Credentials belong in their private login file.
4. Separate verified facts, user-confirmed facts, research suggestions and unverified assertions. Check current primary sources before relying on legal requirements, sponsorship thresholds, portal behavior or time-sensitive claims. Record the verification date.
5. Use the user's current request, then their latest applicable preferences, to select workflow and scope. Use verified candidate evidence for factual claims. Research informs technique and cannot establish qualifications or override factual accuracy. Resolve material source conflicts before proceeding.
