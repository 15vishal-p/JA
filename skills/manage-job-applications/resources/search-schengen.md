# Schengen job search

Coverage is starter guidance. Dedicated country and profession research is pending. Load the user's selected countries and private research before choosing sources.

- Start with the relevant employer's career site, EURES and official national employment services. Use specialist and regional boards to discover leads, then inspect the employer's current requisition.
- Search role synonyms, verified skills, local-language job titles and preferred cities. Record the language actually required by each posting, including requirements in the full description.
- Check each country's employment eligibility separately. A residence document or permission to travel in Schengen does not by itself establish permission to work in every member country. Record unresolved eligibility as a blocker and consult current official sources when necessary.
- Treat sponsor lists and company research as leads. Verify that the specific vacancy accepts the candidate's situation. Historical sponsorship by an employer does not establish sponsorship for the current role.
- Record employer, requisition, official URL, country, city, workplace arrangement, language, salary currency and period, posting status, sponsorship evidence, inspection date and fit gaps.
- Rank candidates for further preparation using the user's preferences and evidence. Keep legal eligibility, required licenses and non-negotiable language requirements separate from numerical fit.

Do not hardcode visa salary thresholds or processing rules from research. Verify applicable official rules at the point they affect a decision.
