# India and Western job search

Synthesized on 13 November 2026 from the supplied *India and Western Job Search* research. Website lists are discovery starting points. Verify current availability and suitability during use. Research conversion rates, ranking multipliers and statutory thresholds have not been independently validated here.

## India

Use Naukri, LinkedIn, Foundit and direct employer careers for broad professional discovery. Consider iimjobs for relevant finance, management and consulting work, and Cutshort, Instahyre or Hirist for relevant startup and technical roles. Select channels by profession and seniority.

Search alternate job titles and verified skills. Review structured profile fields where the user authorizes profile editing, since an uploaded CV may not populate the platform's search fields correctly. Keep actual experience, salary and notice period accurate. Express a negotiable notice period only when confirmed by the user. Record current and expected salary separately, with currency and annual or monthly basis.

## Western markets

Choose the actual country before selecting sources. Broad boards and niche professional boards supplement official employer vacancies.

| Country | Discovery examples | Official eligibility research |
| --- | --- | --- |
| United States | LinkedIn, employer careers, relevant specialist boards | USCIS and current official employer or petition information |
| United Kingdom | Reed, Totaljobs, employer careers | GOV.UK immigration guidance and licensed sponsor register |
| Canada | Job Bank, employer careers | IRCC and applicable official employment or LMIA information |
| Australia | SEEK, Workforce Australia, employer careers | Department of Home Affairs and applicable official visa guidance |

Verify specific vacancy eligibility and sponsorship before treating a cross-border lead as actionable. A sponsor-register entry, past filing or remote label does not establish authorization for the candidate's proposed work location.

For every market, inspect the full JD, deduplicate by employer and requisition, preserve live-source dates and record genuine gaps. Do not turn research recommendations for referrals or recruiter outreach into permission to send messages.
