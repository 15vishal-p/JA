# Slovakia job search

Coverage is starter guidance. Dedicated Slovakia research is pending. Candidate-specific AI Hub preferences remain private user settings.

- Search Profesia, Kariera, EURES, relevant employer career pages and profession-specific channels. Use the employer's current vacancy to confirm a discovered lead.
- Search both English and relevant Slovak title variants. Check whether Slovak is mandatory, preferred or unstated. An English title does not prove an English-only working environment.
- Capture exact city, remote or hybrid limits, contract type, experience, essential qualifications and job expiry. Keep Bratislava and Košice distinct where location matters.
- Preserve the salary's currency, gross or net basis, and period. A posted monthly base amount is not an annual total or a promised final offer. Use the user's confirmed salary-answer rule.
- Record user-confirmed work status with its source and scope. When a legal conclusion is required, verify the current applicable official guidance and the candidate's actual circumstances.
- Resume existing valid drafts and reconcile employer-plus-requisition duplicates before starting another application. Preserve tracker holds and documented exceptions.

FlowCV, two-page exports, geographic priority, notice period and language levels are per-user requirements. Load them from the selected workspace before preparing a CV.
