# CV audit research

Synthesized on 13 November 2026 from supplied *CV Auditing and Scoring Framework* research and reusable AI Hub audit rules. Numerical models in the research are hypotheses unless validated against a named system. There is no universal ATS formula established by this library.

Audit the complete live JD, verified candidate evidence, applicable user rules and exact exported CV version. Keep candidate fit and finished CV quality separate.

| Assessment | Components | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vacancy fit | Skills 30, experience 25, education 15, location and eligibility 15, seniority 15 | Manual evidence-based estimate of candidate alignment |
| CV editorial quality | Qualifications 40, duty alignment 25, concrete evidence 15, clarity 10, readiness 10 | Manual review of the exported document against the JD |

Record awarded points, evidence and gaps for each component. Apply the user's confirmed threshold and exceptions. A high total cannot pass an essential eligibility conflict or a defective export. These scores do not predict an employer's hidden ranking or interview probability.

Check every section for unsupported claims, misleading location, wrong dates, stale employer names, irrelevant skill lists and omitted evidence. Verify role-specific section order, depth and project selection. Inspect extracted text for missing sections or incorrect reading order and inspect every rendered page for legibility, clipping, spacing and page balance.

Record each finding with severity, location, supporting source, impact and required correction. Notify the creator and user of blocking mistakes, then return the exact version for correction and re-export. Approve only the rechecked artifact. A content, layout or attachment change invalidates prior approval.

Keep approved and historically submitted artifacts intact. Flag defective drafts for replacement. Removal of existing files follows the user's actual authorization and the existing archive rules.
