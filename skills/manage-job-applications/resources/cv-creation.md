# CV creation research

Synthesized on 13 November 2026 from supplied *CV Strategy and European Formats* research and reusable AI Hub content rules. Load the candidate's source CV, current preferences and complete JD before drafting.

## Evidence and content

Build an evidence map from important JD requirements to verified employment, projects, education and skills. Use the JD's terminology where it describes genuine work. Preserve actual titles, employers and dates. Quantify scope or results only when supported. Keep unsupported requirements in the internal gaps record.

Use a role-specific headline and summary supported by the candidate's evidence. Remove stale company names and unrelated role summaries. Describe the relevant task, tool or method, and result or scale where known. Distinct bullets should contribute different evidence. Repetition and invented metrics do not improve depth.

## Structure by role

- Accounting and finance usually benefit from substantive relevant employment before projects. Show processes, controls, reconciliations, reporting and tools actually used.
- Technical engineering and career-pivot applications can lead with substantial verified technical projects, followed by concise truthful employment history.
- Data analysis and finance BI need a choice based on the actual JD and strongest evidence. Give relevant methods, tools and results enough space to demonstrate capability.

Use each user's project-selection rules. Never copy another candidate's projects, proficiency levels or accomplishments. Accounting responsibilities can show analytical skills, but describing reconciliation as software development requires evidence of actual software work.

## Export and layout

Follow the employer's requested file format and the user's required editor and page count. Keep standard section headings, readable type and clear reading order. Include contact details in extractable document content. Use accurate residence and distinguish relocation or target location explicitly.

Extract the final text and inspect every rendered page. Correct clipped lines, broken links, stale content, unexpected blank areas and poor page balance. Fill space with relevant evidence when available. Ask focused factual questions when material detail is missing.

Archive each role-specific export as an immutable version. Record its source editor, job identity, version reference and inspection state for the independent auditor. Any later content or layout change creates a new version requiring audit.
